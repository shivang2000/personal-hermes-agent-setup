**Top picks**
- [Mistral releases Shieldstral: 3B open-weights multimodal safety model (Apache 2.0)](https://mistral.ai/news/shieldstral) — Mistral frames moderation as a *policy-adaptive binary QA task*: the policy lives in the prompt, the model returns a calibrated score from a single token. The 3B checkpoint matches guard models up to 7× its size on text safety and sets SOTA on multimodal moderation, fitting on a single 16GB GPU. Practical for anyone shipping a consumer LLM feature without training a per-domain classifier.
- [Maple-Preview: a ternary 20B-A1B MoE that runs at 200+ tok/s on a Mac mini M4](https://deepgrove.ai/maple) — DeepGrove open-sources a 20B/1.5B-active MoE trained natively in ternary weights; HF model card is [deepgrove/maple-preview](https://huggingface.co/deepgrove/maple-preview) (MIT). Reported ~218 tok/s on M4 Mac mini and 127 tok/s on iPhone, with 78.7 avg on LCBv6/AIME/HMMT/GPQA-D. The interesting bit is the design claim: low-precision-first architecture rather than post-training quantization, plus a "Flash" head variant trading some quality for more speed.
- [Uber open-sources ADR — enterprise detection & response for AI agents](https://github.com/uber/ADR) — Four pieces shipped: ADR-Sensor (telemetry from Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, etc., macOS/Linux/Windows), ADR-Bench (300+ tasks, 133 MCP servers, 17 attack techniques), and a two-tier detector. Paper accepted to MLSys 2026. Concrete evidence that production-scale agent security is becoming a first-class category rather than a side concern.
- [Mysk: IP and DNS leaks in WebKit bypass proxy browsers and iCloud Private Relay](https://mysk.blog/2026/08/04/webkit-proxy-icloud-private-relay-ip-leak/) — Three WebKit subsystems (DNS prefetching, WebAuthn Related Origin Requests, WebTransport) bypass the configured proxy and connect directly from the device, exposing the user's real network. Affects Tor/iOS browsers and Apple's own Private Relay; the same researchers ship Psylo 1.3.1 with the fix. Worth a read for anyone running iOS apps that route traffic through a VPN or proxy.
- [Firecrawl/pdf-inspector: fast Rust PDF classifier + extractor (10–50ms detection)](https://github.com/firecrawl/pdf-inspector) — Detects text-based vs scanned PDFs without OCR, then extracts structured Markdown (tables, headings, reading order). Ships Python, Node, and WASM bindings. On opendataloader-bench it's 0.875 overall at 0.47s for 200 docs vs pymupdf4llm's 0.735 at 17.1s. Useful primitive for RAG ingest pipelines that currently pay OCR cost for ~50% of incoming PDFs.
