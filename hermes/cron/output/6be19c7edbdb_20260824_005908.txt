**Top picks**
- [Rust 1.98 has a P-critical stable miscompilation](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/161441) — A regression can emit a null method slot in a compiler-generated vtable for boxed async services, causing safe Rust to dispatch to address zero. It reproduces on 1.98.0 but not 1.97.1 or a later nightly; production users with dynamic async-trait dispatch should hold upgrades or test aggressively.
- [Malware distributed through Android automotive-head-unit firmware updates](https://securelist.com/android-head-unit-malware/121106/) — Kaspersky found DoFun’s privileged TWCore updater installing a multi-stage downloader tied to the BADBOX/MoYu ecosystem. The payload performs ad fraud and turns head units into reverse-proxy nodes; the vendor says it has fixed the distribution weakness.
- [Four AI models collaborated to root an “unrootable” Fire HD tablet](https://ericpardee.github.io/fire-hd-ownership/) — Kimi K3 identified an unpatched Mali CVE in the device kernel, while GLM-5.3 corrected build-offset and page-table assumptions and produced a repeatable root. The detailed case study is a useful look at model handoffs, long-running cyber agents, verification failures, and the practical limits of provider safeguards.
- [ASR leaders may be optimizing for public test sets rather than speech](https://huggingface.co/blog/asr-benchmark-optimization) — Tests across 11 open models found systems reproducing erroneous VoxPopuli transcripts and even supplying numbers removed from the audio. Held-out audio and perturbation probes are increasingly necessary when selecting speech models for production.
- [Dutch regulator fines Uber €825M over automated account decisions](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/21/netherlands-fines-uber-automated-driver-suspensions) — The authority says drivers were deactivated without adequate notice or meaningful human involvement, violating GDPR restrictions on consequential automated decisions. Uber disputes the characterization and will appeal, but builders should treat human-review and challenge mechanisms as core product architecture.

**AI, Agents & Models**

Vendor tests report up to 3.18× H100 throughput, 2.87× on-device speedups, and 57% lower function-calling latency, while greedy output remains identical to the target model.

The key architectural argument is that owning the harness preserves session data, model portability, and workflow control independently of any single model vendor.

Shorter slots reduce stale-price exposure and leader control windows, but double vote frequency at 200ms and leave less latency headroom for voting and leader handoffs—making staged mainnet measurement critical.
