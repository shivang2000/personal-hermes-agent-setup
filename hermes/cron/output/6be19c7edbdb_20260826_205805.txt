**Top picks**

- **AWS to acquire DuckLabs** — [ducklabs.com](https://ducklabs.com/news/2026/08/26/ducklabs-to-join-aws). Mark Raasveldt and Hannes Mühleisen's 30-person team in Amsterdam joins AWS effective early September. DuckDB, DuckLake, and Quack stay MIT-licensed under the DuckDB Foundation; AWS commits to long-term support and a new technical advisory board. Big ecosystem signal: first-class AWS integration for the in-process OLAP engine plus broader distribution.
- **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next weights released (preview of Qwen4)** — [qwen.ai](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.8-flash-next). 125B-param MoE with only 6B active + a separate 51B N-gram embedding table; hybrid Gated DeltaNet + Gated Attention; 262K native context (1M with YaRN). Qwen claims training cost is ~1/9 of Qwen3.7-Plus and reports SWE-bench Pro 62.5, NL2Repo 48.1, CoWorkBench 73.9. Hosted pricing is $0.16 / $0.47 per M tokens — very aggressive for the capability tier.
- **GLM-5.3-Flash: Z.ai confirms `ox-alpha` and open-weights the model** — [z.ai](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3-flash) and [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/china-s-z-ai-made-ox-alpha-stealth-model-that-rivals-deepseek). 320B / 18B-active MoE with hybrid linear+sparse attention and a custom `IndexPool`; hits 57 on AA Intelligence Index at ~$0.045/task and beats GLM-5.2 by wide margins on DeepSWE v1.1 (63.4 vs 46.2) and AutomationBench (48.8 vs 26.2). Notably served at scale on Chinese accelerators via a SGLang-based stack, with Encode–Prefill–Decode disaggregation and W8A8/INT8/FP8 hybrid quant. Weights on Hugging Face; SGLang/vLLM/TokenSpeed supported.
