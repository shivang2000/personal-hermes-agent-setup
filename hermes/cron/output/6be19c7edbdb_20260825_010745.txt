- [NVIDIA Groq 3 LPX on Vera Rubin: 3,431 tok/s at 100K input context](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-nvidia-groq-3-lpx-unlocks-ultrafast-interactivity-at-long-context-on-nvidia-vera-rubin/) (NVIDIA developer blog, 24 Aug) — Deterministic, compiler-scheduled LPU execution with chip-to-chip scheduling at the granularity of 320-byte vectors; eliminates real-time arbitration for tensor-parallel transfers. Artificial Analysis median: 3,431 tok/s at 100K context, 3,382 at 10K, ~4,767 tok/s peak with >20% of runs over 5,500 tok/s. Most consequential inference-engineering artifact since the first Groq LPU launch; decisively changes the cost curve for long-context agents.
- [seL4 confidentiality proofs now complete on AArch64](https://proofcraft.systems/news-2026/#2026-08-21) (Proofcraft / NCSC, 21 Aug; HN 146p) — After 2024 functional correctness and 2025 integrity, Proofcraft has now formally proved seL4 enforces confidentiality on 64-bit Arm. Means AArch64 deployments have a machine-checked mathematical guarantee that an app running on seL4 cannot learn information it isn't authorized to see. Closes the last major formal-verification gap on the architecture that ships in phones, cars, and most cloud Arm instances.
- [IPFS at Shipyard winding down — Protocol Labs funding not renewed](https://ipshipyard.com/blog/2026-the-end-of-ipfs-at-shipyard/) (Shipyard, 24 Aug; HN 224p) — Final day of IPFS-related engineering, maintenance, and infra: 30 Sept 2026. Shipyard has been a core maintainer of IPFS/libp2p/related primitives with ~75M monthly active users via its free gateways. Commenters point to Iroh (built by ex-Protocol Labs devs) as the spiritual successor; concrete implications for anyone running IPFS-backed NFT/file storage or Brave/Unstoppable-Web integrations.
- [Hot Chips 2026: NVIDIA details CUDA-on-RISC-V server profile (with SiFive demo)](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/hot-chips-2026-cuda-targets-risc) (Chester Lam / Chips and Cheese, 24 Aug) — NVIDIA requires server-grade RISC-V CPUs with ACPI (UEFI BRS spec ratified last year), PCIe coherency, peer-to-peer PCIe, vector extensions with predication, and other "two pages" of constraints. SiFive plans a CUDA-capable RISC-V system at the conference. NVLink Fusion lets custom RISC-V CPUs talk to NVIDIA GPUs. Highest-leverage silicon roadmap signal of the week.
