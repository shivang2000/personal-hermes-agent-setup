- [Universal Ruby 4.0 deserialization chain turns `Marshal.load` into RCE](https://www.elttam.com/blog/ruby-4-0-universal-rce-deserialization-gadget-chain) — elttam’s standard-library gadget chain works unchanged from Ruby 3.3 through current 4.0.6. This is not a remotely reachable flaw by itself, but any application that passes attacker-controlled bytes to `Marshal.load` should be treated as command-execution vulnerable and migrated to a constrained format.
