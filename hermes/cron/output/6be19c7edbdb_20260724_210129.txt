**Top picks**

- **[FLUX 3: a single multimodal model that also drives robots (Black Forest Labs)](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-mimic)** — BFL shipped FLUX 3 in early access, a single backbone jointly trained on images, video and audio (no separate encoders stitched together), and showed it can also predict actions to control a real robot arm at Audi. Most striking claim: video+audio fine-tuning caused zero regression in image generation, with the *joint* training — not a stack of specialists — being the architectural bet.
- **[Kimi K3 cyber evaluation: UK AISI + US CAISI joint report (NIST)](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/07/uk-aisi-caisi-preliminary-assessment-kimi-k3s-cyber-capabilities)** — Moonshot AI's flagship (released 16 Jul, open weights due 27 Jul) clears the "frontier cyber-capable" bar in raw exploit development on ExploitBench but is *below* top US models, reaching only step 17/32 on a simulated corporate network kill-chain vs 28.5 for the strongest US models. Crucially, its safeguards did **not** stop it from assisting with offensive cyber tasks during evals.
- **[Mohabi: sandboxing SpiderMonkey inside Firefox with SFI (USENIX OSDI '26)](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/presentation/sharma)** — UT Austin + Stanford + Mozilla + Google + UCSD used software-based fault isolation to pull SpiderMonkey out of the browser process and sandbox it, paying ~24.8% on JetStream / 24.4% on Speedometer. The accompanying x86-64 SFI toolchain is the fastest published (5.9–6.6% SPEC 2017). First credible path to in-process sandboxing for a large, legacy C++ engine.

**AI, Agents & Models**

- **[FLUX 3 base model: 20-second video+audio, native lip-sync, multi-shot chaining (BFL)](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3)** — Generates 720p video up to 20s with synchronized audio, keyframe-to-video, video-to-video with character preservation, multilingual dialogue, and agentic clip chaining. BFL's preliminary side-by-side prefs: 77% over Runway Gen-4.5, 93% over Luma Ray 3.2, 60% over Kling v3 Pro. The "Self-Flow" alignment scheme is the actual paper-worthy contribution.
- **[SANA-Video 2.0: 720p video DiT with hybrid linear attention (NVIDIA Research + Singapore Lab)](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/Video2/)** — 5B and 14B variants; "Hybrid Linear-Softmax Attention" runs gated linear attention most of the time, with periodic softmax anchors at a 3:1 ratio + "Block Attention Residuals" that route block summaries back into later linear layers. 40-step, 720p/5s on a single H100 in 13.06s; ~120× faster than Wan 2.2 14B at the same quality (VBench 84.30).
- **[DARPA + USAF fly an AI-controlled F-16 in-air under VENOM (DARPA)](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2026/darpa-us-air-force-fly-ai-controlled-f-16)** — VENOM autonomy kit is the testbed; ACE-program successor flights are now "human-on-the-loop" (a human can abort, but isn't flying). Goal: a rapid-iteration pipeline for swapping in new combat agents without re-certifying the airframe. Real flights, not sim.
- **[Launch HN: Screenpipe (YC S26) — local screen/audio capture into agent memory](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49024620)** — Records screen+mic locally (no cloud), indexes with OCR/ASR, exposes a searchable memory API to coding/automation agents. Worth watching as a building block for always-on personal agents and as a privacy story (everything stays on-device).
- **[Why Software Factories Fail (HumanLayer)](https://github.com/humanlayer/advanced-context-engineering-for-coding-agents/blob/main/wsff.md)** — Dex from HumanLayer collects the contrarian evidence: PR review quality down, incidents up, bugs/developer up since Jan/Feb (cites Faros AI report); outages attributed to coding-agent mistakes; StrongDM's "lights-off" factory's sparse public updates. Useful counterweight to the StrongDM/OpenAI Symphony narrative.
- **[Show HN: Echo — pool-of-models routing at 1/3 the cost of a frontier call (tracerml.ai)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49026810)** — Routes each prompt across a pool of open-weight models (GLM-5.2, Kimi K2.7, etc.), allocating compute and combining outputs dynamically. Show HN post claims consistent gains over the best individual model in the pool. Interesting test of "no single frontier model" architectures.
