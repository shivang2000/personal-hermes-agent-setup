**Top picks**
- **[Lean kernel soundness bug #14576 — postmortem](https://leodemoura.github.io/blog/2026-8-1-postmortem-for-kernel-soundness-bug-14576/)** (Leonardo de Moura / Lean FRO) — A soundness bug in the Lean 4 kernel let a proof of `False` through because phantom parameters on a nested inductive type escaped type checking during kernel elimination. Discovered via an AI-generated "Collatz disproof," fixed in ~1h; an independent Rust checker (nanoda) had a separate, coincidentally-similar bug. Important for anyone trusting LLM-generated or kernel-checked formal proofs.
- **[Coldcard hardware-wallet RNG flaw — $70M+ drained](https://engineering.block.xyz/blog/predictable-rng-fallback-and-32-bit-reseed-in-coldcard-firmware)** (Block Engineering + Coinkite) — A `#ifndef` instead of `#if` in libngu made COLDCARD's `random.bytes()` fall back to a deterministic MicroPython Yasmarang PRNG seeded from the MCU UID + SysTick + RTC. Mk2/Mk3 firmware v4.0.0–v4.1.9 have *no* secure-element reseed; Mk4/Q/Mk5 add a 32-bit reseed (≈2³¹ candidates). Any seed generated on affected firmware should be treated as burned.
- **[Kimi K3 served on AMD MI355X beats B300 on perf/$](https://www.wafer.ai/blog/kimi-k3-mi355x)** (Wafer.ai) — 8× MI355X at TP8 reaches 952 tok/s/node aggregate at 118 tok/s/stream for 2.8T-param Kimi K3 — ~3.8× a 2-node TP16 B200 build on aggregate, and ~1.45× the perf/$ of B300. The interesting engineering detail: most of the gap closed without custom kernels, just by working around a missing `top_k_renorm_prob` alias in sglang's ROCm sampling branch.
- **[ByteDance Seedance 2.5 — 30-second audio-video joint generation](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5)** — Unified multimodal audio-video model generating up to 30s clips in a single pass with multi-round extension, up to 30 images / 10 video / 10 audio references per generation, and timestamp-level editing.
