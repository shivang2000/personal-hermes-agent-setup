- [Malicious `arrayref` crate removed from crates.io after build-script payload confirmed (Rust Security Response WG)](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/08/20/supply-chain-attack-on-arrayref) — A published 0.3.10 of the widely-used `arrayref` crate was found to ship a `build.rs` that downloads a malicious payload at compile time; Rust yanked it and the advisory ([RUSTSEC-2026-0260](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0260.html)) flags two related crates, `internment` and `append-only-vec`.
