- [Cloudflare fixes cross-tenant data exposure in Containers](https://blog.cloudflare.com/containers-cross-tenant-vulnerability/) — `dm-thin` had `skip_block_zeroing` enabled, so a 4 KiB write could allocate a reused 64 KiB block and expose up to 60 KiB of another tenant’s residual data. Cloudflare re-enabled zeroing, retired existing disks and caches, and reports no evidence of malicious exploitation.
- [Bitget reports a $351.6M backend compromise](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/bitget-says-suspected-north-korean.html) — The attacker allegedly compromised wallet infrastructure, spoofed transaction data, and triggered the authorization path across several chains. Withdrawals were suspended; attribution to North Korean actors remains Bitget’s preliminary assessment while Mandiant and SlowMist investigate.
- [Go experiments with portable, size-agnostic SIMD](https://go.dev/blog/simd-experiment) — Go 1.27’s experimental `simd` package targets AVX/AVX2/AVX-512, Arm NEON, and WebAssembly SIMD from one API, falling back to emulation elsewhere. It is aimed at near-assembly performance without architecture-specific Go assembly; enable it with `GOEXPERIMENT=simd`.
- [Fed proposes the GENIUS Act stablecoin framework](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20260924a.htm) — One proposal requires full backing with assets such as short-term Treasuries, standardized capital/risk controls, and safekeeping rules; another defines the application process for supervised banks issuing stablecoins. Comments close 60 days after Federal Register publication.
