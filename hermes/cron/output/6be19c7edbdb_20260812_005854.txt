- [Stolen reasoning traces from Claude, GPT, and Gemini APIs](https://stolen-thoughts.com) — Researchers at ELLIS Institute Tübingen / MPI-IS show that the encrypted `thinking` blocks returned by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google can be replayed against a weaker, jailbroken sibling model to recover the strong model's full chain-of-thought in plaintext. - [OpenSSH 10.5 / 10.5p1](https://www.openssh.com/releasenotes.html) — Fixes an ssh-agent/session-bind@openssh.com interaction where locked-agent binding refusals let forwarded agents add PKCS#11 tokens and use destination-restricted keys remotely (reported by sn0x-sharma). Also adds a composite post-quantum key type (mldsa44-ed25519, off by default) and moves pubkey-type checks before parsing in sshd. Patches for sftp, scp, and the GSSAPI pre-auth DoS from 10.4 remain in tree. Update servers and CI images.
- [Apple Silicon + macOS VMs: 11–16× faster llama.cpp via a Metal capability shim](https://github.com/trycua/cua/blob/main/blog/gpu-passthrough-macos-vms.md) — Cua's "Lume" project ships a process-scoped Metal capability shim that lies about supported GPU family (Apple 5 → Apple 9) and max threadgroup memory (32 KB → 64 KB), letting llama.cpp select SIMD-matrix/bfloat16 paths inside a macOS guest. On M1 Ultra: TinyLlama 1.1B 11.08× faster prompt / 16.36× faster gen; Gemma 4 12B QAT 7.20× / 14.54×; Meta's Muse Glimmer 30B 7.55× / 8.87× — prompt processing hits 98–99% of bare-metal. Pure software, no VFIO needed.
- [Riot Platforms signs ~$9.1B, 20-year deal with Anthropic for AI compute](https://www.coindesk.com/) — Bitcoin miner pivots part of its Corsicana, TX data-center capacity to AI workloads. Pre-market RIOT +20%. The second large BTC-miner → AI-HPC pivot after Core Scientific; materially compresses the pure-play crypto compute thesis and points to where marginal ex-mining MW will land.
- [Mojo 1.0 released](https://news.ycombinator.com/) — Modular's superset-of-Python systems language (now with the MAX platform) reaches 1.0 after a long max-version period. Notable for AI infra: unified Python interop, MLIR-based compilation, first-class accelerator targeting, and a stable ABI for kernels. Worth a serious look for teams hand-writing GPU/CPU kernels who don't want to maintain a C++/Python split.
