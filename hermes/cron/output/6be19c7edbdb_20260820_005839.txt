**Top picks**
- **OpenRouter is joining Stripe** — Stripe is acquiring OpenRouter; per the official post, OpenRouter keeps its name, mission, and API surface unchanged. For anyone routing multi-model traffic this is the most consequential infra consolidation of the cycle — neutral inference routing now sits inside the largest payments layer in AI. ([openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/openrouter-is-joining-stripe/))
- **Go 1.27 released** — Generic methods on types (e.g. `Rand.N[Int intType](n Int) Int`), struct-literal keys for embedded fields, and full function-type inference in composite literals/channel sends. Also: `encoding/json/v2`, `crypto/mldsa` (post-quantum signatures), `simd/archsimd`, size-specialized allocation, and `goroutineleak` profiler. ([go.dev](https://go.dev/blog/go1.27))
- **Ornith-1.5: closed-loop self-improvement** — Open-weights family (397B MoE / 35B MoE / 9B dense) trained with a single GRPO loop that jointly optimizes task generation, scaffold construction, and solution rollouts; task reward = validity × frontier difficulty × novelty. 397B hits 86.1 Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 56.0 DeepSWE, on par with Claude Opus 4.8. ([ornith.ai](https://ornith.ai/ornith_1_5.html))
- **Cerebras CS-4 rack-scale system** — Three WSE-3 Turbo wafers per system with 2 µs wafer-to-wafer latency, claims up to 30× faster token generation vs GPUs and 10× throughput/watt vs CS-3; "deploy infrastructure, then compute" via separable PowerRack + modular compute backpacks. ([cerebras.ai](https://www.cerebras.ai/cs4))
- **Cloudflare revises remote Spectre defenses on Workers** — Aug 19 deep-dive from Schwarzl & Pedersen covering new Spectre gadgets, remote timers, co-location primitives, and the mitigations added to V8 isolates. Mandatory reading for anyone running untrusted multi-tenant JS/Wasm at edge. ([Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/?search=spectre))
