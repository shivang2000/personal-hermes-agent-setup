**Top picks**
- [Microsoft fixes maximum-severity Entra ID RCE](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/microsoft-entra-id-flaw-cvss-100.html) — CVE-2026-69836 is an unauthenticated deserialization flaw rated CVSS 10.0. Microsoft says the cloud service is fully mitigated with no customer action required—and corrected its bulletin to clarify that the vulnerability **was not exploited in the wild**.
- [NVIDIA research puts the agent harness—not just the model—at center stage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/nvidia-just-showed-that-the-harness-not-the-ai-model-is-now-the-real-hero/) — Fine-tuning around tool interaction, state management and execution policy reportedly made weaker models reliable on complex agent tasks. The practical implication: evaluate model+harness combinations; swapping in a larger model may be less valuable than training the control loop.
- [Cloudflare demonstrates a remote Spectre leak in production Workers](https://blog.cloudflare.com/revisiting-spectre-attacks-on-workers/) — Researchers achieved an arbitrary-address side channel leaking up to 12 bits/s at 99% accuracy despite coarse timers and production noise. Cloudflare has patched Dynamic Process Isolation, deployed the V8 Sandbox and added in-process isolation; it found no evidence of real-world exploitation.
- [Expired DNS dependency exposed military-base call metadata](https://lina.sh/blog/hijacking-e164-arpa) — A researcher bought an expired nameserver domain delegated for three `e164.arpa` ENUM zones, gaining potential control over VoIP routing for Diego Garcia, Ascension Island and Saint Helena. Hundreds of thousands of ENUM lookups exposed phone numbers, timestamps and resolver IPs; the researcher returned NXDOMAIN, deleted logs and did not intercept calls.
- [Qwen3-TTS serving reaches sub-50 ms p95 first audio](https://nari-labs.com/blog/qwen3-tts-speed-cost-frontier/) — Nari Labs reports 10 RPS with no playback underruns on one H100 SXM. Its scheduler independently interleaves Talker, Code Predictor and Codec work, uses deadline-aware batching, captures the fixed 15-step predictor loop as a CUDA graph and caches codec state; implementation and benchmarks are open source.
- [NVIDIA reportedly agrees to a $6B Poolside licensing deal](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-pay-poolside-6-billion-181448803.html) — Bloomberg reports NVIDIA will license Poolside’s model-development technology and offer jobs to more than 100 employees. It resembles a non-acquisition talent/IP transaction, underscoring how strategically valuable coding-model training infrastructure has become.

**AI, Agents & Models**
- [A 125M-parameter piano-autocomplete model running on iPhone](https://simedw.com/2026/08/20/midi-autocomplete/) — The model generates roughly 108 notes/s by predicting a complete structured note per transformer pass rather than autoregressing each MIDI attribute. Aggressive deduplication, explicit onset/duration representation, scheduled sampling and DPO mattered more than simply scaling the dataset.
- [Huzzah experiments with persistent, declarative specifications for coding agents](https://www.danielvaughn.dev/posts/huzzah/) — Instead of disposable English chat, the editor stores terse pseudocode as the durable expression of human intent. It is early, but the design addresses prompt repetition, lost provenance and weak correspondence between requirements and generated code.

**Software Engineering & DevTools**
