**Top picks**
- **[Stripe in talks to acquire OpenRouter for ~$10B (WSJ)](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/stripe-talks-acquire-openrouter-potential-215104525.html)** — OpenRouter raised at $1.3B in May; ~7.7× markup. OpenRouter is the routing layer that lets enterprises mix frontier + open-weight models; Stripe already runs payments on top of it. A Stripe-owned LLM router turns the payments giant into the routing fabric of the agent economy.
- **[AMD × Cerebras: disaggregated inference](https://www.cerebras.ai/press-release/amd-and-cerebras-announce-industry-leading-ultra-low-latency-and-high-throughput-ai-inference)** — Helios (AMD Instinct, high-throughput prefill) and Cerebras Wafer-Scale Engine (low-latency decode) stitched into one inference workflow, claiming 5× tokens/sec/W. Cerebras Cloud H2 2026. Direct shot at NVIDIA's vertically integrated stack.
- **[Microsoft open-sources MLVC (Multi-platform Learned Video Codec)](https://github.com/microsoft/MLVC)** ([blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/linuxandopensourceblog/announcing-the-open-source-release-of-ml-video-codec-mlvc/4539875)) — 18.3M-param neural codec claiming >70% MOS BD-rate gain over hardware HEVC, ~100 FPS on Apple/Intel/Qualcomm NPUs. Ships CoreML, ONNX (OpenVINO), QNN exports, plus a C++ entropy coder. First serious open-weights video codec with on-device NPU paths.
- **[PyTorch Monarch lands on AMD ROCm](https://pytorch.org/blog/bringing-pytorch-monarch-to-amd-gpus-single-controller-distributed-training-on-rocm/)** — single-controller distributed runtime (Meta) ported to AMD Instinct MI325 with fault-tolerant recovery; the same post shows 96.16% scaling efficiency on 1024-GPU MI325 for FP8 DeepSeekV3-671B. Closes one of the biggest CUDA-only gaps.
- **[India I4C orders GitHub to remove Bitchat](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/government-orders-github-to-remove-bluetooth-based-chat-app-bitchat-over-security-concerns-jack-dorsey/article71262049.ece)** — Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre cites the Bluetooth-mesh P2P app as enabling "anonymous communication" during internet shutdowns. Jack Dorsey pushes back; Bitchat surfaces on [Radicle](https://radicle.network/nodes/rosa.radicle.network/rad%3Az2v9tRJz1oknFAqCSY5W5c76nVvm6). First real test of "git hosts can't remove what you sign" as a hosting-defiance primitive.
