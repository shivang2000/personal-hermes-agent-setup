**AI, Agents & Models**
- [Liquid AI releases DSpark draft models for LFM2.5](https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/lfm25-dspark) — Roughly 300M-parameter draft models use parallel drafting, a Markov-style sequential head, and confidence-scheduled verification for speculative decoding. Liquid reports up to 3.18× H100 and 2.87× on-device speedups, with upstream support in SGLang and llama.cpp and identical greedy outputs by construction.
- [ASR benchmarks may reward transcript memorization rather than listening](https://huggingface.co/blog/asr-benchmark-optimization) — Tests across 11 open ASR models found some high-scoring systems reproduced erroneous VoxPopuli and LibriSpeech references even when audio was altered or contradicted the transcript. The proposed disagreement, silencing, and ambiguity probes are useful additions to speech-model evaluation pipelines.
- [Open Qwen3-TTS serving reaches sub-50 ms p95 time-to-first-audio](https://nari-labs.com/blog/qwen3-tts-speed-cost-frontier/) — Nari Labs reports 10 RPS on one H100 while sustaining real-time playback, using dynamic silence trimming, staged codec chunk sizing, and optimized batching. The implementation and benchmark are open sourced, though the cost and latency numbers remain vendor-reported.

**Cloud, Infra, Data & Security**

It can materially cut observability ingest, but teams need enough Collector memory and consistent routing so every span for a trace reaches the same decision point.
